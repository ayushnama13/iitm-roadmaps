# CS1202 — Discrete Mathematics II (graphs, algebra, number theory)

| | |
|---|---|
| Tier | A |
| Phase (order) | 2 |
| Do this first | CS1200 |
| Estimated effort | 8-10 weeks, ~5 h/week |
| Unlocks | CS2320, CS2800, CS6111 (cryptography), CS6030 |

## Roadmap

1. Graph theory basics: paths, cycles, trees, bipartite, Euler/Hamilton, connectivity, Menger, matchings, planarity.
2. Algebraic structures: groups, rings, fields.
3. Number theory: gcd, Bezout, modular arithmetic, CRT, Fermat.

## Study plan (week by week)

Lecture numbers are positions in the main playlist above. Click a number to open that video.

| Week | Lectures | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | MIT 6.042 lectures on number theory (GCD, modular arithmetic, RSA) | Extended Euclid, modular inverse, CRT, Fermat/Euler; implement RSA toy in Python. |
| 4-6 | MIT 6.042 graph lectures + NPTEL Graph Theory (alt) | Graph basics, connectivity, trees, matchings, colouring. Prove Hall's theorem. |
| 7-9 | NPTEL IIIT-B Discrete Maths (alt) algebra part | Groups, rings, fields, lattices. Do Rosen/Gallian exercises. |
| 10 | MIT 6.042 counting lectures | Advanced counting + generating functions. Solve 15 Project-Euler-style counting problems. |

## Practice / proof of understanding

Prove Euler's characterisation, Hall's theorem and Fermat's little theorem in your own words. Implement extended Euclid and fast modular exponentiation.
