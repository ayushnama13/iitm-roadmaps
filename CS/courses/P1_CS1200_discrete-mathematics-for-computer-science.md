## Roadmap

1. Logic first: propositional and predicate logic, truth tables, resolution.
2. Then proofs: direct, contradiction, induction (weak and strong). Spend the most time here; every later theory course is proofs.
3. Sets, relations, equivalence relations, functions, countable vs uncountable (Cantor).
4. Counting: permutations, combinations, inclusion-exclusion, pigeonhole, recurrences.
