## What your IITM course sheet says

### From the PDF: DA6300

**Official description:** Quantum computing hardware has matured to the extent that there are some scientific experiments for which  results using  the currently available hardware outperform the results from using state-of-art classical supercomputers. On the  other  hand, machine learning (especially deep learning) models have been increasingly successful in a variety of  domains spanning  across science, economics and engineering. This course aspires to cover this interplay between quantum  computing and  machine learning from both the directions:  1. Quantum ML: Machine learning models built with quantum algorithms which leverage quantum computing  principles  2. ML for Quantum: Use of (classical) machine learning in enhancing the quantum computing software stack

**Official syllabus (trimmed):**

> 1. Quantum computing foundations. (3 weeks) Qubits – Gates – Relevant quantum mechanics foundations –
>  Circuit identities – Phase kickback – Quantum circuits – Teleportation demo - Dynamic Circuits - Transpilation -
>  Error mitigation
>  2. Variational quantum algorithms and applications. (3 weeks) Optimization problems - Quantum Encoding
>  schemes (Basis, Angle, High dimensional, Amplitude, QRACs) - Eigenvalue problem and Quantum phase
>  estimation
>  algorithms - Variational eigenvalue solver - Examples of evaluating ground state energy - Other example
>  applications
>  in finance, chemistry and biosciences.
>  3. Quantum Machine Learning (5 weeks) Variational Algorithms for machine learning - Quantum-classical hy-
>  brid learning algorithms - Quantum Neural Networks(QNN) - Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQA) - Quantum
>  Kernel Estimation (QKE) - Quantum Support Vector Machine (QSVM) - Quantum Generative Adversarial Net-
>  work (QGANs) - Quantum Convolutional Network (QCNN) - Quantum Graph Convolutional Network (QGCNN) -
>  Quantum Recurrent Neural Networks QRNN) - Quantum Autoencoders, Quantum Boltzmann Machines (QBM).
>  4. Machine learning applications to quantum computing stack (3 weeks) ML for compilation - ML for error
>  correction - ML for error mitigation - ML for quantum code assistant

**Official textbooks:** 1. Nielsen and Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information, Cambridge University Press  2. Cerezo et al., Variational quantum algorithms, Nature Reviews Physics  3. Relevant research papers in the topics of interest.
